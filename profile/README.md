## Kimiko

Kimiko is a private AI note taker for meetings and notes on Mac and Windows.
It transcribes your calls without a bot joining, writes a summary with
suggested next steps, and lets you dictate into other apps. Speech and AI run
on your computer by default; cloud providers are optional.

AI apps on your computer can also use your meetings, notes and tasks through
Kimiko's local MCP server (`app.kimiko/kimiko`). It's off until you turn it on.

- Website: https://kimiko.app
- Docs: https://docs.kimiko.app
- Support: https://kimiko.app/support/ or info@kimiko.app
